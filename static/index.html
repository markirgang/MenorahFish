📖 ANIMATRONIC MENORAH FISH & CANDLE ROBOT ARM OPERATING MANUAL

Complete guide for show stage operation, menorah candle lighting routines, 5-DOF robot arm controls, and Gemini AI voice/vision integration.
Step 1

🕎 Hanukkah Menorah Show & Candle Lighting

  • Quick Candle Lighting: Click any button (1 through 8 Candles) in the Candle Lighting Selector card.
  • Autonomous Sequence: The 5-DOF robot arm automatically positions its claw candle to extinguish existing flames and light the selected side candles alongside the central Shamash!
  • Extinguish All: Click 🛑 Extinguish All to turn off all menorah side flames.
Step 2

👄 Animatronic Fish & Lipsync Mechanics

  • L298 Ch A (Mouth DC Motor): Drives the realistic opening mouth mechanism synchronized to vocal audio frequencies.
  • L298 Ch B (Neck Swing Motor): Triggers a 3-second outstretched neck swing motion during peak song moments.
  • Tail Wag & Spotlights: Controls reverse polarity tail wagging and stage spotlight dreidel servos.
Step 3

🦾 5-DOF Robot Arm Manual Control

  • Joint Sliders: Manually adjust Base Yaw (J1: -90° to +90°), Shoulder (J2), Elbow (J3), Wrist (J4), and Gripper Claw (J5).
  • Quick Gestures: Trigger preset arm motions including Rest Position 🏠, High Five ✋, Nod Yes 👍, and Shake No 👎.
  • Claw Flame: Toggle the flickering LED flame on the arm's claw candle.
Step 4

🎙️ Gemini AI Voice, Mic & Multimodal Vision

  • Live Mic: Click 🎙️ Start Voice Mic to talk directly to the fish using web audio VU level metering.
  • Command Examples: Ask the fish: "Light 4 candles", "Tell a Hanukkah joke", or "Sing a song".
  • Camera Feed: Select USB camera or Pi CSI camera feed to allow Gemini vision analysis of audience and surroundings.
Step 5

🎵 MP3 Playlist & Animatronic Lipsync

  • Upload MP3s: Use 📁 Upload MP3 to add local Hanukkah or custom music tracks to the playlist.
  • Web Audio Engine: Click ▶️ Play & Sync to run live Web Audio FFT analysis that drives fish mouth actuation in realtime!
  • Lipsync Tuning: Adjust Vocal Lipsync Gain, Music Volume, and Tail Beat Sensitivity sliders.
Step 6

🔧 Servo Calibration & Hardware Wiring

  • Wiring Tab: Review interactive SVG circuit schematics for Raspberry Pi GPIO, PCA9685 PWM driver (0x40), and L298N dual H-bridge motor connections.
  • Servo Trims: Switch to 🔧 Servo Calibration view to fine-tune zero offset trims and save settings permanently.
  • Export PDF/CSV: Download schematic documentation or Python hardware control scripts using top header buttons.
